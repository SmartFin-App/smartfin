Personal expense tracking app for Thai users
SmartFin
Automatic Expense Tracking with Thai E-Slip Recognition and an AI Financial Coach
Thai payments end in an e-slip, yet budgeting apps still make you type every expense by hand. SmartFin reads the slips for you.

Snap the slip, skip the typing

A vision model reads the merchant, the amount in Baht and the date. You confirm before it saves.

Auto-Detect from the gallery

New e-slips from K PLUS, SCB EASY, Krungthai NEXT and other banking apps are found and offered for review.

Bills that remind you

Subscriptions and recurring bills that never produce a slip, with a reminder the day before they are due.
